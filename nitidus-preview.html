Why it is awkward

A car interior is not a small room

It is a set of narrow gaps arranged around furniture that does not move. Household vacuums are built for open floors, and the places a car actually gets dirty are the places a full-size head cannot reach.

  • Geometry

    Seat rails, belt anchors and the channel between seat and console are all narrower than a standard vacuum head.

  • Power

    Cars are parked where sockets are not. A mains lead reaches the driveway or it does not reach at all.

  • Debris

    Grit and pet hair work into the weave rather than sitting on it, so suction has to lift rather than sweep.

What this does not cover

Accidental damage is not covered — a dropped unit or a cracked housing is not a fault. The filter is a consumable: it washes rather than replaces, but it will not last the life of the vacuum and a new one is not a warranty claim. Battery capacity falls with use, and a cell holding less charge at the end of two years is wear rather than failure. Anything else that stops it working is ours to fix.

Questions

The things people ask

When will it arrive?

Ordered before 2pm on a working day, it leaves the same day. After that, the next working day. Royal Mail Tracked 48 then takes two to three working days, occasionally four to the Highlands and Islands.

If it is out of stock the product page says so and will not take the order. We would rather lose the sale than take your money and email you a delay a week later.

Will it actually lift pet hair, or just move it around?

Hair worked into a seat weave needs lift rather than sweep, which is what the 18,500 Pa and the brush head are for. It will clear dog hair from fabric seats and carpet mats in one or two passes.

What it will not do is beat a full-size upholstery machine on a heavily matted seat that has never been cleaned. Nothing at this size will.

How long does a charge last, and is that one car or two?

Thirty-two minutes on a full charge. A single car interior — footwells, seats, boot and the console — takes about twelve to fifteen minutes at a normal pace, so one charge covers two cars with room to spare.

A full charge from empty takes about two and a half hours over USB-C.

Can I charge it in the car?

Yes. It charges over USB-C from any 5V source, including a car adapter or a power bank. It will not run while charging from a car socket, because the draw while running is higher than a 12V adapter reliably supplies.

Do I have to keep buying filters?

No. The HEPA filter washes under a tap and goes back in once it is completely dry. There is no cartridge and no subscription.

It is still a consumable — after a year or two of regular use it will stop recovering and want replacing. Spares are sold at cost.

How loud is it?

72 dB at a metre, which is roughly a domestic vacuum and quieter than a forecourt machine. Loud enough that you would not use it at seven in the morning outside a neighbour's window, quiet enough to hold a conversation over.

Will it fit in the door pocket?

310 × 78 × 90 mm and 680 g. It fits most door bins and every glovebox we have tried, and the case takes the nozzle and brush head alongside it in a boot side pocket.

Is it any good for wet mess?

No, and using it for one will damage it. It is a dry vacuum: spilled coffee, wet mud and melted snow are all jobs for a cloth first. Let a wet patch dry fully before vacuuming it.

Can I use it for anything other than the car?

People mostly do. Pushchairs, dog crates, the stairs, keyboards, the inside of a laptop bag, campervans and boats. It is built around car geometry rather than restricted to it.

What if it is not right for me?

Send it back within 30 days, used or unused, and we pay the return postage. There is no restocking fee and nothing to argue about — if it did not do what you hoped, that is a good enough reason.
